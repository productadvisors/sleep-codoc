What it does

Five common reasons sleep breaks. We help surface which may be yours.

Insomnia

Trouble falling or staying asleep

Sleep apnea

Breathing pauses that fragment sleep

Parasomnias

Sleepwalking, night terrors, REM acting-out

Restless legs

An urge to move that delays sleep

Limb movement

Repeated leg movements through the night

Strongest today for sleep-apnea signals — the rest flag patterns worth discussing with a specialist.
